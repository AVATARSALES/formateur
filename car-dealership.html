Analysis Grid — Car Dealership Sales Interview

Ousmane (salesperson) × Ms Loto (family SUV prospect) — Identify the areas for improvement and propose your own approach

BTS / BUT level — 5 criteria
Context: Ousmane welcomes Ms Loto at the dealership. She is looking for a spacious, economical family SUV for her daily trips with her children. Ousmane offers her the Vectus Premium at €31,580. Ms Loto negotiates down to €27,000. Ousmane grants a 9.5% discount (€3,000) in under 2 minutes.
Instructions: For each criterion, identify the area for improvement in the video, then write your own approach.
Criterion Scenario 1 — Areas for improvement Your approach
1. Making contact
Hint
Ousmane knows Ms Loto's name from the start, but what does he do to build a human connection before talking about cars? Look for a "warming" moment in the dialogue.
Your turn
How would you have broken the ice? What personal question would you have asked before talking about the vehicle?
2. Needs discovery
Hint
Ousmane asks about daily use and budget. But does he ask about Ms Loto's current vehicle, her frustrations, her safety criteria for her children?
Your turn
List 3 additional questions you would have asked to better understand Ms Loto's real need.
3. Vehicle presentation
Hint
Ousmane presents the Vectus's technical features (engine, consumption, options). But does he use Ms Loto's own words ("family", "children", "shopping") to link the features to her concrete needs?
Your turn
Rephrase one of Ousmane's product arguments by linking it directly to a need expressed by Ms Loto.
4. Handling the price objection
Hint
Ms Loto offers €27,000 (vs €31,580). Ousmane grants a 9.5% discount in under 2 minutes, without arguing the value or getting anything in return. What message does this send to the customer?
Your turn
Write the response you would have given to the €27,000 objection. What value argument and what concession in return would you have proposed?
5. Closing and farewell
Hint
Ousmane presents the discount with "This is an offer to grab right now!" Does he notice Ms Loto's buying signals before closing? Does he propose a next step or a follow-up?
Your turn
How would you have closed the sale? What closing technique would you have used and what follow-up would you have put in place?

Discussion Questions — Group Analysis (BTS / BUT)

Q1. Ousmane grants a €3,000 discount in under 2 minutes. If you were Ms Loto, what more would you have asked for — and why would you have been right to do so?
Hint: when a salesperson gives way this quickly, what perception does it create in the buyer?
Q2. Ousmane says: "It's a very competitive price compared to other brands." Is this a value argument or a positioning argument? What is the difference?
Hint: a value argument is about the customer, a positioning argument is about the market.
Q3. Ms Loto mentions her children and their school activities. Ousmane never uses this word in his argument. Write a sentence that works "your children" into the presentation of the Vectus's boot.
Hint: "With 520 litres of boot space, you can load the school bag, the sports bag and the pushchair without folding the seats down."
Q4. Ousmane suggests a test drive during the meeting. What could he observe during the drive to strengthen his argument on the way back?
Hint: Ms Loto's spontaneous reactions (comfort, quietness, visibility) are buying signals to rephrase.
Master's level — 9 criteria
Context: Ousmane × Ms Loto — Vectus Premium SUV at €31,580. Ms Loto negotiates down to €27,000. A 9.5% discount is granted in under 2 minutes with no argument and nothing in return.
Instructions: For each criterion, identify the area for improvement, then write your alternative approach. The hints steer your analysis — the full argument is yours.
Criterion Scenario 1 — Areas for improvement Your approach
1. Making contact and building rapport
Hint
Ousmane goes straight to the vehicle need. No personal question, no warming, no coffee. What impact does this have on trust?
Your turn
Write your first 3 welcome sentences, including a personalised touch and some human warmth.
2. Quality of discovery (SPIN method)
Hint
Ousmane asks Situation questions (use, budget) but no Problem questions (frustrations), Implication questions (consequences) or Need-Payoff questions (projection). What depth of discovery is missing?
Your turn
Write 1 question for each SPIN type, adapted to Ms Loto's context.
3. FAB argumentation (Feature → Advantage → Benefit)
Hint
Ousmane states features ("5L/100km", "150 horsepower") without turning them into customer benefits. Where does he stop in the FAB chain?
Your turn
Turn "5L/100km combined cycle" into a complete FAB argument for Ms Loto.
4. Use of the customer's vocabulary
Hint
Ms Loto says "family", "children", "school activities", "shopping". Ousmane uses "generous space", "folding seats", "large boot". Does he pick up her key words and reuse them?
Your turn
Rephrase the boot presentation using Ms Loto's exact words.
5. Handling the price objection
Hint
9.5% discount in under 2 minutes, with no resistance, no value argument, nothing in return. Analyse the impact on Ms Loto's perception of the price and on the dealership's margin.
Your turn
What objection-handling sequence would you have applied? (Acknowledge → Question → Argue value → Concession in return)
6. Negotiation and concessions in return
Hint
Ousmane grants the discount without asking for anything in return (no trade-in, no financing commitment, no accessories). Calculate the value of what he leaves on the table.
Your turn
Propose 3 concessions Ousmane could have asked for in exchange for the €3,000 discount.
7. Making the most of the test drive
Hint
The test drive is mentioned but not exploited. On return, Ousmane asks "What did you think?" — a closed question that opens nothing. How can the test drive be turned into a closing lever?
Your turn
Write 3 post-drive questions that turn Ms Loto's reactions into sales arguments.
8. Closing technique
Hint
"This is an offer to grab right now!" — false urgency. Does Ousmane use a closed alternative, a summary of benefits, or a projection into everyday use? Which technique would be more suitable?
Your turn
Write your close using the closed-alternative technique or the benefits summary.
9. Soft skills and sales stance
Hint
Active listening is present but partial (no rephrasing). Empathy is visible but there is no firmness on price. Communication is adapted but the argument is too technical. Which soft skill is missing to transform the meeting?
Your turn
Identify Ousmane's dominant soft skill and the one he lacks, and explain how this gap affects the final outcome.

Discussion Questions — Group Analysis (Master's)

Q1. Ousmane asks no SPIN "Implication" or "Need-Payoff" questions. Write one Implication question and one Need-Payoff question suited to Ms Loto's context (children's safety, fuel budget).
Hint: Implication = "If your current car has no Isofix, what impact..." · Need-Payoff = "If you saved €80/month on fuel, how..."
Q2. Calculate the margin Ousmane loses by granting a 9.5% discount with nothing in return. Then calculate what he would have kept by negotiating in-house financing (margin on the loan) + an accessories pack (mats, roof bars).
Hint: direct margin lost = €3,000. Estimated financing margin = €800–1,200 over 48 months. Accessories pack = €400–600 gross margin.
Q3. Ms Loto says "I'm ready to negotiate a little" and Ousmane replies "What price did you have in mind?" — he hands negotiating power to the customer. What alternative question would have kept the initiative on the seller's side?
Hint: instead of asking for the customer's price, ask what matters to her beyond price (financing, trade-in, equipment).
Q4. If you were the dealership manager watching this interview, which 3 priority coaching points would you raise with Ousmane in the debrief?
Hint: think in terms of skills (discovery), stance (firmness) and process (systematic concessions in return).
