Discussion Questions — Model Answers (BTS / BUT)

Q1. In scenario 1, Célia makes 3 successive concessions. If you were Arhan, what more would you have asked for?
Model answer points: Go further: extra discount, free services or longer support. Three concessions in a row signal wide room to manoeuvre; a savvy buyer would push harder since nothing is asked in return.
Q2. In scenario 2, Arhan refuses a 2-year commitment… What intermediate length could Célia have proposed?
Model answer points: Idea: an intermediate length (e.g. 18 months) or 2 years with an exit/review clause at 12 months, in exchange for something (testimonial, logo, reference). Adapt to the exact dialogue of the scenario.
Q3. What is the precise moment in scenario 2 when Arhan sees the price as an investment?
Model answer points: The moment Célia quantifies the impact of the "+10% retention" on revenue, before moving on to the discount: the price is then compared with the gain, not with the budget.
Q4. In an ideal negotiation, what would Célia's first 3 actions be?
Model answer points: 1) Quantify the ROI. 2) Propose the framework herself with returns (logo, testimonial). 3) Secure a long commitment (3 years) in exchange for a capped discount.

Discussion Questions — Model Answers (Master's)

Q1. Which scenario best applies "separate the people from the problem" (Fisher & Ury)?
Model answer points: Scenario 2: Célia rephrases Arhan's interests (growth, retention) before defending her own, and builds a joint solution rather than a power struggle.
Q2. Calculate the revenue secured in each scenario.
Model answer points: Method: base price × (1 − discount) × length × licences. Scenario 2 secures 3 years with a 12% discount; scenario 1 only 2 years, with cumulative discounts (volume + preferential rate + 5%). Scenario 2 generates the most net value, before counting the marketing value.
Q3. SPIN counter-argument to the "flexibility" objection (refusal of the 2-year term).
Model answer points: S: "You use your CRM every day…" P: "Is switching tools easy?" I: "Migration, training, data loss: what would it cost?" N: "Would a commitment guaranteeing price and support give you more peace of mind?" Adapt to the scenario's dialogue.
Q4. Which scenario would Arhan's CEO approve most easily?
Model answer points: Scenario 2: best value-for-investment ratio (demonstrated ROI) and the security of a 3-year commitment, with a discount justified by what is received in return.
